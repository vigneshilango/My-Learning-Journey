How to measure (Navy body-fat method)
  • Neck: Just below the Adam's apple — tape sloped slightly down in front, horizontal in back. Don't flex your neck.
  • Waist: At belly button (navel) level — tape horizontal around your abdomen, relaxed (don't suck in).
  • Height: Stand straight, no shoes — measure barefoot in inches.
  • Hip (female): At the widest part of your hips/buttocks — tape parallel to the floor.

Use the same spots every week for consistent tracking.
